### 🔬 [home-security-lab](https://github.com/NumanBhat1/home-security-lab)
A personal cybersecurity lab built on an Apple Silicon Mac (UTM) and a Windows desktop, used for hands-on offensive and defensive practice.

- 🔎 **Full vulnerability assessment** of a Metasploitable 2 target: reconnaissance with Nmap, Nikto, WhatWeb, and enum4linux
- 💥 **Manual exploitation of 9 OWASP Top 10 web vulnerability classes** in DVWA, each documented with payload, proof of concept, business impact, and remediation
- 📄 A consolidated, **professional VA report** (19 findings, risk-rated and mapped to CVSS)
- 📊 Growing **SIEM / detection engineering** section: Splunk, Sysmon, and detections mapped to MITRE ATT&CK
